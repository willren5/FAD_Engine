# CLA acceptance record template

Copy and complete this record for the actual contribution. Keep sensitive
identity/authority documents privately; the public PR may reference a private
record without publishing personal data. This unfilled template is not consent.

```text

PROJECT-LICENSOR.md SHA-256:

Rights holder: legal entity or individual, or verified private-record reference
Representative and authority: private-record reference where appropriate
Repository:
Contribution commits / immutable patch / version:
Affected paths:
Third-party material and exclusions: list, or an express statement of none
Acceptance author / account and date:

I am the rights holder or am authorized to bind the identified rights holder.
I have read and expressly accept the identified agreement for this contribution,
including its grant to FAD Records to modify, commercially use and sublicense
the contribution in paid, closed-source FAD Synth and other permitted products.
I have disclosed third-party material and obtained necessary employer/client/
coauthor authority. My independent material outside the stated scope is excluded.
```

Maintainer review: confirm hashes, identity/authority, code scope and third-party
compatibility. Record the decision and any required separate permissions. A
changed PR head needs a scope check; acceptance of one patch is not an unlimited
grant of all future work.
